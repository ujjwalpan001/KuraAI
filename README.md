## What KuraSewa does

Small shops in Nepal get most of their orders in chat, and they miss sales when nobody replies at night, during a rush, or in the language the customer used. KuraSewa replies for them, 24/7.

| | |
|---|---|
| 💬 **One inbox, every channel** | WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and comments, Facebook Messenger, email and a website chat widget, all in one dashboard. |
| 🗣️ **Talks like a local** | Understands and replies in Nepali, Roman Nepali and English, matching how each customer writes. |
| 🛍️ **Shows products, takes orders** | Live stock, prices and sizes in the chat, with photos. Customers order by tapping buttons or just typing ("M size ma order garchu"). |
| 📸 **Photo product search** | A customer sends a photo; KuraSewa finds that product in the shop's catalog. |
| 💳 **Payment verification** | Reads eSewa, Khalti and bank payment screenshots, matches the amount and reference to the order, and catches a payment that was already used. The owner approves with one tap and the customer gets a receipt. |
| 🎙️ **Hey Kura** | The owner asks by voice, "How much did we sell today?", and gets the answer spoken and shown from the shop's live data. |
| 🙋 **Knows when to call a person** | Never guesses. When it doesn't know, or the customer is upset, it hands the chat to the team. |
| 👥 **Team access** | Owner, Manager and Support roles, so staff only see what they need. |
